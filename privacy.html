Sales Link Operations

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 22, 2026 • Effective Immediately

1. Overview

Sales Link ("we", "our", or "platform") is an independent retail auto-loan sales execution and workflow management platform accessible at https://saleslink.in. This Privacy Policy outlines how authorized user information and customer application data are collected, processed, retained, and safeguarded across all connected banking and showroom workspaces.

2. Information We Collect

When authorized personnel or guest users interact with Sales Link, we collect:

  • Identity & Profile Information: Name, work email address, and profile photo provided via Google Single Sign-On (OAuth), official designation, and contact number.
  • Operational Loan Data: Customer applicant names, phone numbers, vehicle models, requested loan amounts, underwriting stages (Inbound, Login, FI, Sanction, Disbursed), and daily sales End-of-Day (EOD) submissions.
  • Immutable Security & Audit Logs: Access timestamps, session tokens, and administrative action logs (such as supervisor delegation and impersonation audits in the platform's immutable audit collection) strictly for compliance, role-based boundary verification, and fraud prevention.

3. How Information Is Used

Data collected within Sales Link is utilized exclusively for:

  • Authenticating sales hierarchy members (Zonal, Area, Branch Sales Managers, Relationship Officers, and Dealership Consultants).
  • Routing inbound showroom leads directly to local branch underwriting pipelines.
  • Tracking retail auto-loan file turnaround times (TAT) and generating operational performance dashboards.

We do not sell, rent, monetize, or disclose customer financial or personal data to third-party commercial entities or advertising brokers.

4. Data Storage, Security & Access Boundaries

All application traffic is encrypted in transit using 256-bit SSL/TLS protocol. Application records are stored within Google Cloud Firestore and protected by server-side tenant-scoped security rules that isolate branch workspace data, preventing cross-branch or cross-tenant leaks.

5. Data Retention & Customer PII Deletion Procedure

Customer loan file records and operational performance entries are retained during the active pipeline lifecycle and for regulatory auditing periods. To request the deletion or anonymization of customer Personally Identifiable Information (PII):

  • Branch Sales Managers may soft-delete completed or cancelled files directly from the dashboard, removing them from active views.
  • For permanent regulatory purging or erasure of customer phone numbers and names, authorized supervisors may submit an erasure request to admin@saleslink.in. All deletions are audited and processed within 30 days.

6. Google API Services User Data Policy

Sales Link's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
